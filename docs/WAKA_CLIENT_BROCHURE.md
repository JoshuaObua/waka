# WAKA — Your Property. Fully Managed. Always in Your Pocket.

*"Waka" means Home in Luganda — and that's exactly what this platform protects: the place
people live, work, and build their future.*

---

## The Big Idea

Running a property — whether it's a single rental house or a portfolio of estates spanning
several countries — comes with the same headaches: rent that's hard to track, tenants who are
hard to reach, maintenance that falls through the cracks, and paperwork that piles up.

**WAKA replaces all of that with one connected system.** One place where you register your
properties, onboard your tenants, collect rent, bill utilities, manage maintenance, talk to
vendors, and see exactly how your business is performing — in real time, from anywhere.

Whether you own one building or manage hundreds of properties for other owners, WAKA grows
with you.

---

## Who WAKA Is Built For

- **Individual landlords** who want to stop chasing rent with spreadsheets and phone calls
- **Property management companies** running dozens or hundreds of properties for multiple
  owners, who need one dashboard instead of a hundred
- **Estate developers and institutions** managing residential, commercial, and mixed-use
  portfolios across cities and countries
- **Housing cooperatives and HOAs** that need transparent, auditable shared-cost management

No matter the size, every client gets their own secure, private space on the platform — your
data is never visible to anyone outside your organization.

## What Makes WAKA Powerful

### 1. One Platform, Every Type of Property
From a single rental house to a shopping mall, a hostel to a hotel, an office tower to an
industrial park — WAKA understands the difference. Properties, buildings, floors, and units are
all organized in a clear hierarchy, with custom categories for residential and commercial
portfolios, so your reports and billing always reflect the real structure of your business.

### 2. Tenants Onboarded the Right Way, Every Time
Tenants can be added by your team or can register themselves through the mobile app, the web,
or even a basic phone via USSD. Every tenant goes through identity verification before they're
ever assigned a unit — protecting you from disputes and bad actors. Document uploads, guarantor
details, and approval steps are all built in, so nothing slips through.

### 3. Leases That Manage Themselves
Create a lease once and let WAKA handle the rest: automatic rent escalation, renewal reminders,
late-payment penalties, and notice periods — all configured to your rules, not a generic
template. When a lease is up for renewal, WAKA tells you before your tenant has to ask.

### 4. Get Paid, However Your Tenants Want to Pay
Mobile money, bank transfer, card, cash, cheque, QR code, even a basic feature-phone USSD
session — WAKA accepts it all and reconciles every shilling automatically. No more guessing
which tenant paid what. Every invoice, receipt, and payment is tracked, matched, and recorded
the moment it happens.

### 5. Know Exactly Who Owes You Money — and Get It Back Faster
Arrears don't hide in spreadsheets anymore. WAKA automatically ages every overdue balance,
sends reminders on a schedule you control, and gives your team a clear collections dashboard
so nothing — and no one — gets forgotten.

### 6. A Built-In Bank for Your Business
Every tenant, owner, and vendor can have their own digital wallet inside WAKA — for deposits,
top-ups, refunds, and transfers — all backed by a transparent ledger. Combined with full
expense tracking and budget monitoring, you always know exactly where your money is.

### 7. Fair, Transparent Utility & Service Charge Billing
Water, electricity, gas, and shared service costs are calculated automatically — split fairly
across tenants by usage, square footage, or occupancy — and billed without manual spreadsheets
or disputes. Tenants see exactly what they're paying for.

### 8. Maintenance That Never Falls Through the Cracks
A tenant reports a broken tap through the app, the web, or even a phone call — WAKA logs it,
assigns the right technician or vendor, tracks the job against a service-level promise, and
notifies everyone when it's done. Nothing gets lost in a notebook or a WhatsApp thread again.

### 9. Trusted Vendors, Managed Professionally
Every contractor and supplier you work with has a profile, a contract, a performance history,
and a payment trail — so you always know who's reliable, who's overdue on a job, and who
deserves more of your business.

### 10. Visitors Managed Like a Modern Estate
Pre-register guests, issue digital badges or QR passes, and know exactly who is on your
property and why — with a full check-in/check-out history for peace of mind and security.

### 11. Every Document, Safely in One Place
Leases, IDs, certificates, insurance papers, inspection reports — all securely stored, version
controlled, and instantly retrievable. Expiry reminders mean you're never caught off guard by
a lapsed certificate or an outdated contract.

### 12. Communication That Actually Reaches People
Email, SMS, push notifications, USSD, even WhatsApp — WAKA reaches tenants and owners however
they prefer, automatically, for everything from a payment reminder to a maintenance update.

### 13. Real Answers, Not Just Raw Data
Occupancy rates, rent collection performance, profit and loss by property, maintenance
turnaround time, vendor costs — all available as clear dashboards and reports you can export
and share with owners, investors, or auditors in a click.

### 14. Built for Trust and Accountability
Every action on the platform — every payment, every approval, every change — is logged and
auditable. You can always answer "who did what, and when," which matters for your own peace of
mind and for regulatory or investor scrutiny.

### 15. The Right Access for the Right People
Owners, managers, accountants, maintenance staff, security, and tenants each see only what's
relevant to their role. You decide exactly who can approve a refund, edit a lease, or view
financial reports — and you can change it any time.

### 16. Reach Every Tenant — Even Without a Smartphone
This is what sets WAKA apart in markets like ours: tenants without a smartphone or data plan
can still register, check their balance, pay rent, and report a maintenance issue using a
simple USSD code on any phone. No one is left out of the system.

### 17. One App, Built for Everyone Who Touches Your Property
Dedicated, simple mobile experiences for tenants, owners and managers, maintenance teams,
visitors, and support staff — each one designed around what that person actually needs to do,
not a one-size-fits-all app that confuses everyone.

### 18. Ready to Grow With You
WAKA is built to scale from a single rental unit to an estate portfolio with thousands of units
and hundreds of tenants — without slowing down, without breaking, and without you needing to
change systems as your business grows.

### 19. Secure by Design
Bank-grade encryption, strong authentication, and strict data isolation between organizations
mean your business information — and your tenants' personal data — stays private and
protected at every step.
